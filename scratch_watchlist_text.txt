How to use this list
Check LinkedIn Jobs and the official careers page 2–3 times per week.
Prioritize roles posted within the last 7 days when possible.
Search for Software Engineer, SDE I, Backend Engineer, Full Stack Engineer, Product Engineer, Node.js, TypeScript, Platform and Microservices roles.
Apply on the company's own careers/ATS page, then look for an employee or recruiter for a referral.
Track company, role, posting date, application date, referral, recruiter and status in a spreadsheet.
Current-source note: 
Current searches confirm that some companies in this list have live engineering hiring. For example, Toast lists engineering roles in Bengaluru and Chennai, Incresco lists Full Stack roles in Coimbatore/Bengaluru, and KovaiSoft lists Full-Stack React/Node roles in Coimbatore. These openings can change after this document is created.
