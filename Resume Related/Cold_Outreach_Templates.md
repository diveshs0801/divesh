## 📋 Quick Dos and Don'ts

### ✅ DO:
- Personalize EVERY message with company-specific details
- Mention a specific product, feature, or technical challenge they're solving
- Keep LinkedIn connection notes under 300 characters
- Follow up exactly once after 5-7 days if no response
- Apply through the careers page BEFORE or ALONGSIDE the cold outreach
- Share your GitHub link (your code speaks louder than words)

### ❌ DON'T:
- Copy-paste the same message to everyone
- Send more than 2 follow-ups total
- Mention salary expectations in the first outreach
- Write walls of text — keep initial messages concise
- Sound desperate ("I really need this job")
- Bad-mouth your current or previous employer
